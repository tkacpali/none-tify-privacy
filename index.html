Privacy Policy

None-tify for Android · Effective from: 24 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Nothing leaves your phone – None-tify doesn't even have permission to access the internet. It reads notifications only to sort them: people and calls get through right away, everything else arrives in a summary. Whatever it stores is encrypted on your phone only, deletes itself after 7 days, and you can wipe it with a single tap.

1. Who we are

The app is developed and operated by Waterio s. r. o., Brečtanová 6544/12, 900 27 Bernolákovo, Slovakia, Company ID (IČO): 54 128 463, registered in the Commercial Register of the Bratislava III City Court, Section Sro, File No. 156265/B. Contact: info@waterio.sk.

2. What the app does with notifications

Once you grant Notification access, the app sees notifications from other apps as they arrive and decides, using rules on your phone:

  • let through immediately – calls, alarms, messages from people and one-time codes; nothing from their content is stored,
  • hold and deliver in a summary – everything else (e.g. shops, parcel delivery, email, social media) at times you choose.

Decisions are made by a fixed set of rules inside the app – not by a server and not by artificial intelligence.

3. What data the app stores

  • Held notifications: which app sent it, title, text and time – so you can see them in the summary and archive. They may include names of other people (e.g. an email sender); these also stay on your phone only. For messages from people that it holds back (e.g. during Quiet time or from an app you excluded), it only remembers which app they came from, when and how many – never the name or the text.
  • Your settings: rules, summary times, Quiet time.
  • Statistics: only counts of interruptions (how many notifications arrived and how many were let through), no content.

Not stored: content of messages from people, one-time codes, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID. There are no user accounts.

4. Where the data is stored

  • Only on your phone, in an encrypted database (the key is protected by the Android Keystore).
  • The app has no internet permission, so it technically cannot send data anywhere.
  • No analytics, ads, tracking, cloud or accounts.
  • The database of held notifications is excluded from Google backup, so it doesn't end up in your Google account backup either.
  • We (Waterio s. r. o.) have no access to this data – we can't see it and can't restore it.

5. How long

  • Held notifications: 7 days (you can choose 3, 7, 14 or 30 days in settings), then deleted automatically.
  • Settings and statistics: as long as you keep the app or until you delete them.
  • The "Delete everything" button erases all data immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

6. Who we share data with

No one. We don't sell, share or send any data from the app – and without internet access it isn't even possible.

7. Permissions and why we need them

PermissionWhy
Notification accessSo the app can see and sort notifications. You turn it on manually in Android settings and can turn it off there at any time.
Post notificationsSo it can send you the summary and warn you if it stops working.
Do Not Disturb accessSo held notifications don't even beep briefly. Calls and people always get through.
Run at startupSo it reschedules summaries and Quiet time after your phone restarts.
Briefly keep the phone awakeSo the regular check (is the app running? is it time for the summary?) also runs while the phone is asleep – for a few seconds, without access to any data.

The app does not request access to the internet, contacts, location, microphone, camera or files.

8. Payments

You buy the app on Google Play. Payment is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. From Google we receive only sales reports (e.g. order number, country, price, any refund) which we need for accounting and tax. We keep them for as long as the law requires (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

9. When you email us

If you email us, we use your address, name and message only to reply (our legitimate interest in handling your request, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep messages for no more than 2 years after our last contact. Our mailbox is hosted by Webglobe, s. r. o. (Yegon).

10. Your GDPR rights and how to use them

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object and to withdraw consent.

  • Data in the app is controlled by you alone: you see it in the archive, erase it with "Delete everything" and can turn off notification access in Android settings at any time. We don't have this data, so we can't provide or delete it for you.
  • Data we hold (emails, sales reports): write to info@waterio.sk. We reply within 1 month.
  • You can also contact a supervisory authority: the Slovak Office for Personal Data Protection, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava, dataprotection.gov.sk, or the authority in the country where you live.

11. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). Since we collect no personal data, we hold no data about children.

  • What gets through and what is held is always decided by the rules on your phone, not by AI.
